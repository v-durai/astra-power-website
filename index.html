Corporate Overview

Tier-One Engineering Quality, Built for Fast Mobilization

Astra Power Technical Services L.L.C - S.P.C is an Abu Dhabi-licensed technical services provider delivering specialized testing, commissioning, diagnostics, and field supervision for high-voltage (HV) and extra-high-voltage (EHV) substation installations.

Founded and led by OEM-certified engineering leadership with over two decades of experience in the GCC power and oil & gas sectors, Astra Power delivers tier-one engineering quality, safety governance, and fast mobilization across the UAE.

Specialized Core Competencies

Deep Expertise Across the Substation Lifecycle

From pre-commissioning through energization and long-term protection governance.

Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS)

Complete pre-commissioning, testing, maintenance, and energization handovers for 132kV, 220kV, and 400kV installations (Siemens 8DN8, 8DN9, 8DQ1).

Advanced Diagnostics & HV Testing

High-Voltage (HV) AC withstand testing, High-Frequency / UHF Partial Discharge (PD) analysis, Point-on-Wave controller (PSD02) testing, and SF6 gas handling.

Protection Schemes & Substation Automation

Scheme verification, busbar stability, restricted earth fault (REF), and protection relay calibration (Siemens, Reyrolle, Schneider/Areva, GE).

Site Governance & Technical Advisory

Production of Method Statements (MOS), Site-Specific Risk Assessments, Inspection & Test Plans (ITP), third-party witness representation, and root-cause failure analysis.
